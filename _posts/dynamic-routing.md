In 2010, after a series of tense encounters with its neighbors, Georgetown formed the _Georgetown Community Partnership_ to negotiate its place in the neighborhood. This committee has the authority to limit the construction of new housing, determine what can be renovated, how many students admitted, and has been the driving force behind programs like SNAP that have demolished campus culture.

Despite initially promising to involve students, the GCP consists primarily of citizens associations and their elected officials in Foxhall, Burleith, and Georgetown. GUSA, the only group representing Students, is apportioned a handful of seats and brushed aside as the University lays down to the demands of wealthy homeowners.

## Erasure of Campus Culture

Georgetown used to be renowned as _the_ place to have a party in DC. For over 100 years, students could enjoy any of the 300+ bars in the neighborhood (including the one in Healy's basement). Students recognized that to excel academically, they also had to excel at cutting loose afterwards.

The teetotaller campus we know now is a recent fabrication, led in large part by traditionalist crusader President DeGoia.
